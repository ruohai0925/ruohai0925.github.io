I'm Ruohai, and I work at the intersection of Computational Fluid Dynamics and AI.

This blog is a space for me to think out loud about where CFD is headed — how AI is changing the way we build solvers, teach fluid mechanics, and deliver simulation results to people who need them.

## What I Write About

- **AI + CFD**: How large language models and AI agents are reshaping the CFD workflow, from mesh generation to post-processing.
- **Software Architecture**: Rethinking CFD software design for an AI-first world — moving beyond monolithic solvers toward composable, verifiable building blocks.
- **Education**: What it means to learn and teach CFD when AI can write solver code faster than most engineers.
